Org Chart Builder — Instructions

Getting Data from Workday

Step 1
Log in to Workday and click the search bar at the top of the page.
Step 2
Search for My Team Employment Data and select the result listed under Reports (not Tasks or People). This is the preferred export — it includes the manager hierarchy the tool needs. If you can't find it, search for My Org Chart instead. Both formats work.
Step 3
The report loads showing your team. In the top-right corner of the report, click the Excel icon and choose Excel (.xlsx) to download the file.
Step 4
Click ⬆ Load Workday in the toolbar (or use the Upload button on the welcome screen). Select the .xlsx file you just downloaded. The chart builds automatically.
Root person prompt: If the tool asks "Who is the top of this org?" after uploading, select the correct person from the dropdown and click Confirm. This happens when the file contains multiple potential root nodes.

Navigating the Chart

Scroll / Pan
Use scrollbars or click and drag on the background to pan around the chart.
Zoom − / +
Step zoom in 10% increments (range 20%–150%). Or hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) and scroll with the mouse wheel.
Fit
Scales the full chart to fit the visible window. Use this before taking a screenshot or exporting.
V / H Sliders
Compress or expand vertical spacing (gap between a manager and their reports) and horizontal spacing (gap between sibling branches) independently. Drag left to compress, right to expand.
Search
Type a name or title to find people. Click a result to scroll to and highlight that node on the chart.

Editing the Chart

Click a node
Opens the context menu. Options: set a color state, toggle layout, collapse/expand children, edit name or title, move to a different manager, or remove the node.
+ button
Appears at the bottom-right of a node on hover. Adds a new direct report under that person — useful for open or budgeted roles not yet in Workday.
⇆ / ⇅ button
Appears at the top-right of a node on hover. Cycles that node's direct reports through three layouts — see Layout Modes below.
◀ Move Left / Right ▶
In the context menu, reorders a person among their siblings — changes their left-to-right position within their manager's team.

Layout Modes

Horizontal
Direct reports displayed side by side. Best for managers with a small number of direct reports.
H+Sidebar
Sub-team leads go horizontal; individual contributors stack vertically on the right. Best for mixed teams where some reports have their own sub-trees and others don't.
Vertical
All direct reports stacked in a column. Best for large teams or when horizontal space is limited.

Color States

Default
Standard filled role — white background, navy border. Used for all active employees.
Budgeted / Open
A role that is approved or budgeted but not yet filled. Use for open headcount or future positions.
Reporting Change
A person or role moving to a different part of the org — useful for illustrating pending restructuring.
Hold
A role or person that is paused — hiring on hold, backfill delayed, or a position under review. Gray background signals it's inactive without removing it from the chart.

Saving Your Work

Important: Any edits you make — colors, layouts, added roles, name corrections — only persist if you explicitly save. Closing the browser tab without saving will lose your changes.
💾 Browser Save
Saves everything (colors, layouts, spacing, zoom) to your browser's local storage. The chart reloads automatically the next time you open this file in the same browser on the same computer. Use this frequently while working.
⬇ Export JSON
Downloads a .json file with the complete chart state — all people, colors, layouts, and custom additions. Use this to back up your work, transfer to another computer, or share with someone else. At the end of each session, export a JSON as a portable backup.
📂 Browser Load
Manually restores the most recent browser save. Useful if you've made changes and want to revert to your last save.
⬆ Import JSON
Restores a previously exported JSON file, fully restoring the chart exactly as it was saved — on any computer.
Sharing with someone else: Send them both the claude-orgchart.html file and your .json export. They open the HTML file, click Import JSON, and select your file. Browser Save is not transferable between computers.

Exporting for Presentations

🖼 Export PNG
Renders the full chart as a high-resolution PNG (2× retina quality) with a white background and no UI controls visible. Download and paste directly into PowerPoint or Teams. Requires an internet connection.
Screenshot tip
If Export PNG is unavailable offline, use your OS screenshot tool (Windows: Win+Shift+S / Mac: Cmd+Shift+4). The layout toggle and + buttons are invisible when not hovering, so the capture will be clean. Click Fit first to get the full chart on screen.
